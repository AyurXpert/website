1. Overview

This guide covers admin.html — "Master Control" — the hospital-level administration dashboard used by a tenant's own super admin or department admin. Every section here is scoped to that one tenant's own data; nothing shown or edited here affects any other hospital on the platform.

1.1 Roles & Access

RoleAccess
super_adminFull access to every section, plus super_admin-only actions (Promote to Admin, some approval steps).
dept_adminFull access to the same sections, except a small number of super_admin-only actions noted where they occur below.
Both roles land here by default Both super_admin and dept_admin are routed to admin.html on login (see ROLE_HOME in js/config/constants.js).

1.2 Sidebar Navigation

The left sidebar is an icon-rail that expands to show labels on hover (52px collapsed → 248px expanded). It's organised into four groups: Operations (Statistics, Accounts), Organisation (Human Resources, HR & Credentials, Departments, Infrastructure, Packages), Account (Subscription, Feature Modules, ABDM Bridge), and Quick Links (shortcuts to reception.html, opd-admin.html, bed-admin.html, ncism-compliance.html, Monthly Report, NCISM Reports).

2. Statistics

The default landing section. Shows today's key numbers at a glance: Patients Today, Revenue Today, Beds Occupied, Pending Approvals, Active Staff, Active Depts.

Two compliance banners appear here for NCISM-regulated institutions (Teaching Hospital/College): an OPD attendance banner (today's patient count vs. the NCISM-mandated daily target, colour-coded green/amber/red) and an IPD bed compliance banner (flags departments whose bed count has drifted out of the NCISM ratio).

A subscription expiry banner also appears here — see Section 13 (Troubleshooting) if it's showing when you don't expect it, or not showing when you do.

3. Accounts

Five KPI cards (revenue breakdown, pending bills, etc.) giving a financial snapshot of the hospital. This is a read-only summary — actual billing work happens in reception.html, dispensaryPOS.html, and finance.html (see the separate Financial Management user guide for those).

4. Human Resources

Two sub-tabs: All Staff (a flat list of every staff member) and Hierarchy (an org-tree grouped into 12 department sections, including NCISM position ladders for Teaching Hospital/College tenants).

  • Login Access approvals — new staff who signed up via signup.html appear here as pending until a super_admin/dept_admin approves them.
  • Promote to Admin — super_admin only. Elevates an existing staff member to dept_admin.
  • Designation (All Staff) — choosing a new designation in a staff member's row opens Save designation? showing the old and the new one; Save writes it (recorded with who and when), Cancel or Esc puts the old one back. A dept_admin changes only ordinary designations — of staff in their own department when they are department-scoped, never their own, never another admin's. Positions that carry powers (Medical Director, Principal, Medical Superintendent, Deputy MS, Nursing Superintendent / Matron, Deputy NS, PK In-charge, Finance Manager — and the Finance Manager / Nurse Manager login roles) are given or removed only by the super_admin; they show as "(Super Admin only)" or the dropdown is locked with the reason. A change that would give or take away a department-scoped person's "My Department" access (e.g. PG scholar → Assistant Professor) is also the super_admin's.
  • Scope to Dept / "My Department" — super_admin only (All Staff → 🏥 Scope to Dept, or Departments → Dept Admin → Change). A scoped account gets the "My Department" page only if it is an active super_admin, a dept_admin, or a doctor who is not a PG scholar / intern; a scoped accountant, pharmacist or trainee does not. Invites from a dept_admin cannot carry a department scope or a powerful position, and a request for one shows "Super Admin approves" in Login Access.
  • Relieve (All Staff and Organisation Staff) — replaces Terminate. Only an active super_admin, Medical Director or Medical Superintendent sees the 📤 Relieve button. Relieving needs a reason type (Resigned, Retired, Transferred, Contract ended, Deceased, Absconding or Terminated (disciplinary)) and a reason; it locks the person's sign-in at once and moves them to Relieved Staff. An admin, a senior position (Medical Director, Principal, Medical / Nursing Superintendent and deputies, PK In-charge, Finance Manager, Nurse Manager) or an account with a department scope is relieved only by the super_admin. Nobody relieves their own account, and no approval request is needed any more.
  • Relieved Staff (Reactivate) — lists relieved staff with the reason type ("Terminated" appears only for the disciplinary type; records from before this change show "Relieved – reason not recorded" with their old reason), and below them Inactive (not relieved) accounts set inactive by the old "Mark Inactive". Reactivate follows the same rule as Relieve (super_admin, Medical Director or Medical Superintendent; admin / senior / scoped accounts: super_admin only) and unlocks the sign-in. A dept_admin no longer relieves or reactivates.
  • Block / Suspend / On Leave (the HR page, hr.html → View Profile) — the same rule: shown only to the super_admin, Medical Director or Medical Superintendent (admin / senior / scoped accounts: super_admin only). "Mark Inactive" has been removed — use Relieve. A relieved account is reactivated only from Relieved Staff.
  • Reset MFA — in a staff member's profile modal, a super_admin can remove another user's MFA factor if they're locked out (mandatory for super_admin/dept_admin/finance roles). Use this before resorting to any database-level fix.

4.1 NCISM Staffing Compliance — Invite to Fill a Position

For Teaching Hospital/College tenants, the Hierarchy sub-tab's NCISM Staffing Compliance panel shows a required vs. actual ladder for every position NCISM mandates (Professor/Associate Professor/Assistant Professor per teaching department, Medical Superintendent, Matron, Staff Nurse, and every other Schedule I/Schedule XX post) — computed live from your UG intake and real department/staff records, so it always reflects your current sanctioned capacity with no manual re-seeding needed.

Whenever a row shows a shortfall (a red "−N" gap), a + Invite button appears next to it. Click it to:

  1. Confirm or change the designation (if the row covers more than one, e.g. "Professor / HOD") and the HMS login role it should get.
  2. Enter the candidate's phone and/or email (name is optional, for your own reference only).
  3. Click Generate Link — you'll get a one-time join link pre-scoped to that exact department and designation.
  4. Copy the link and share it yourself via WhatsApp, SMS or email (there is no automatic email dispatch).

When the candidate opens the link, their organisation, role and department are already locked in — they only fill in their own name, contact details and password. Their account lands in Login Access approvals exactly like any other signup, pending your final approval; once approved, the ladder's gap for that position automatically closes. A small "🔗 N pending" note appears next to a row if an invite has already been sent and is still awaiting signup — so you don't accidentally send a duplicate.

4.2 Registration No., Qualification & Licence on Bills

Bills and prescriptions print the prescriber's Registration No., the dispensing pharmacist's qualification and Registration No., and the organisation's drug / pharmacy licence no. They print only values your organisation has verified.

  1. Staff enter their own details — Account → 👤 My Profile → Professional Registration: Registration No., issuing council / board and qualification, then Submit for verification (doctors, PG scholars / interns, pharmacists, nurses, lab and therapy staff). They should upload a copy of the certificate under Official Documents. A Registration No. typed at staff signup arrives here as a pending submission too.
  2. An admin verifies — the 🏅 HR & Credentials icon in the left rail, or Human Resources → 🏅 Credentials (Reg. No.), opens hr.html's Credentials tab. Registration on bills — pending verification shows each submission next to what prints now. ✔ Verify makes it print from that moment; ✖ Reject… needs a reason, which the staff member sees in My Profile. Nobody can verify their own submission — another super_admin / dept_admin must.
  3. Values on file from before verification keep printing and are labelled "On file (before verification)". They appear in the same pending list with ✔ Verify as on file (marks them verified without changing them), or use Correct below if they are wrong.
  4. Changes — a staff member who submits a change goes back to "Pending"; bills keep printing the previously verified value until the change is approved. A prescription keeps the Reg. No. it was stamped with when it was written.
  5. Correct (in Printed on bills now) lets an admin fix a staff member's printed values directly from the original certificate — a reason is required and recorded. You cannot correct your own.
  6. HPR ID (ABDM Healthcare Professional Registry, 14 digits) goes through the same submit → verify flow. A change submitted without an HPR ID keeps the verified one.
  7. Medical certificates are numbered MC/<year>/<n> per organisation when issued (doctors only) and can be reprinted from the patient's visit history, My Patients or the 🧾 Visits & Bills search — every print after the first is marked “Duplicate copy No. N”.
  8. Frozen on every signed document — a pharmacy bill (pharmacist + licence), prescription, medical certificate, discharge summary, MHA consent and OT record keeps the identity that was verified when it was created or signed. A later verification or correction changes new documents only. Documents made before this existed show the current verified details.
  9. Drug / pharmacy licence no. — Organisation Profile → Drug / pharmacy licence no. (optional). Only a super_admin can change it.

Every submission, verification, rejection, correction and licence change is recorded in the audit log (who, when, old → new value).

4.3 Who Admits a Patient (set this up before go-live)

  • Normal path — a consultant doctor advises the admission in the consultation; Reception admits from 🛏️ Admission Requests. The admitting doctor is always the advising doctor and the department is the advised one — Reception cannot change either. Trainees (PG scholars, interns) never advise an admission.
  • Without an advice — only an active super_admin, on ipd.html with a written reason (for example "on behalf of Dr X (on leave)" while a doctor is on leave). Recorded and audited. A dept_admin admits only from a doctor's advice.
  • Emergency (night / casualty) — staff with the designation Resident Medical Officer (RMO) or Chief Medical Officer see "🚑 Emergency admission" on ipd.html and admit under the consultant. Give those designations in HR → All Staff. Enter each department's on-call consultant in the 24×7 Duty Roster (shift "On-Call"): the RMO's list shows today's on-call consultant first. If nobody is rostered, the RMO may choose a consultant of the department or the Medical Superintendent; the admission is then marked "consultant not on roster" and the Medical Superintendent and that department's HOD are alerted (a department with no HOD: the Super Admin). The consultant confirms the admission on doctor.html within 24 hours.
  • Assign every consultant to their OPD (OPD Management) and give each department its HOD (department scope): the emergency list reads both.
  • Refused admission — when a patient refuses an advised admission, Reception records the reason ("Refused admission against medical advice"); the advising doctor is alerted and a signature form is printed (Original, reprints "DUPLICATE COPY · No. N").
  • The admission counter — Reception sets the payer on each advice (🛏️ Admission Requests → "💳 Payer & insurance"). For private insurance the insurance case (insurer, TPA, policy, pre-auth status, approved amount, the insurer's letter) is opened before the bed and goes with the patient at admission. An insurance patient may be admitted while the pre-auth is pending (until it is requested it shows "⚠ Pre-auth not requested"); a rejected pre-auth makes the patient self-pay and alerts every active receptionist; a deposit that was waived stays waived and the person who waived it is alerted.
  • Bed type — a bed of another type than the doctor's room type preference needs a reason at admission (recorded, audited, sent to the doctor). General Ward = general / male / female general ward / dormitory beds; Semi-Private = semi-private / twin-sharing; Private = private / deluxe / suite; ICU / HDU = ICU / HDU beds.
  • Deposit — a self-pay, private-insurance or corporate patient gets the bed after a deposit of more than ₹0 (government schemes: no deposit). Only an active Finance Manager, the Medical Superintendent (designation) or the super_admin can waive it, with a reason. The Super Admin's own admission without an advice with ₹0 counts as the Super Admin's waiver. Every waiver is in the top card of Admin → 💰 Accounts → Admission deposit waivers (by month, with the month's count; also "🛏️ Deposit waivers" in the admin menu; the Medical Superintendent also sees it on doctor.html). The Finance Manager reads the same report (read-only) in Finance → 💵 Revenue Tracking → 🛏️ Deposit waivers. The suggested deposit and its percentages (Fee Admin → Admission Advance Policy) are changed only by the super_admin, and every change is recorded. An emergency admission is admitted at once and shows "deposit due" until the deposit is paid or waived.
  • Every morning at 08:00 (IST) the server: expires an advice not admitted within 7 days (the advising doctor is alerted), alerts Reception 2 days before when the insurer has not decided the pre-auth, and reminds the consultant and the Medical Superintendent (no MS: the Super Admin) of an emergency admission not confirmed after 24 hours (once a day).
  • Alerts — the 🔔 bell on doctor.html, reception.html and admin.html lists each person's own alerts only (nobody reads another person's alerts).
  • "AyurXpert has been updated — Reload" — after an update a page left open shows this message; press Reload when the form you are on is saved (the page never reloads by itself).

8. Subscription & Plan

This section shows the tenant's own billing relationship with AyurXpert — what plan they're on, when it expires, and (for Teaching Hospital/College tenants) lets them request more UG/PG capacity. Everything shown here is configured platform-wide by AyurXpert's platform admin (see the separate Platform Admin guide) — a tenant admin cannot change their own plan, pricing, or GST rate from this page; they can only view their current state and submit an NCISM capacity request.

8.1 Current Plan & Billing

Shows the plan name and status chips, the expiry/trial-end date with a colour-coded days-left indicator (amber ≤30 days, red ≤7 days or expired), and — for a paid plan with a billing cycle set — the billing cycle (Monthly/Annual) and the fee, including a GST breakdown (e.g. "₹4,999 + 18% GST = ₹5,899").

Nothing to renew from here There's no self-service upgrade/payment button — the page shows a "contact support@ayurxpert.com to upgrade or renew" note. Renewals and plan changes are applied by the platform admin from subscription.html's All Tenants tab.

8.2 Staff Usage

Active staff count against the plan's Max Staff Users limit, with a progress bar that turns amber at 70% and red at 90% of the limit.

8.3 NCISM Capacity Plan (Teaching Hospital / College only)

This card only appears for tenants of type Teaching Hospital or College. It lets the admin request a new UG intake tier and/or add PG department seats — a platform admin then reviews and activates the request.

  1. 1
    Select a UG Intake Tier — cards show each available intake size (e.g. 60/100/150/200 students/year) with its fee and, once a GST rate is configured, a small "incl. GST ₹X" line. The tenant's current tier is marked "Current".
  2. 2
    Select PG Department seats (optional) — tick a department checkbox and enter the number of PG students. Each PG seat in a clinical department raises that department's required IPD beds by 4 (non-clinical departments add no beds). On a first-ever activation those beds are created for you; on a later upgrade you add them yourself in Quick Setup (see the note below).
  3. 3
    Review the total request fee — a live-updating total at the bottom shows the base fee, the GST%, and the GST amount, adding up UG tier fee + (PG seats × per-seat fee).
  4. 4
    Submit Request — the request goes to the platform admin's NCISM Requests queue as pending. Nothing on the tenant's own OPDs/departments/beds changes yet.
  5. 5
    While pending — the card shows the submitted request's details instead of the selection UI, with a "✕ Cancel Request" option if the tenant changes their mind before it's reviewed.
  6. 6
    On approval — for a first-ever activation, the tenant's OPDs, departments and a starting set of IPD beds are all created automatically. For a later upgrade, only the capacity numbers change (UG intake, PG seat counts, OPD daily target) — no beds are created; you add the extra beds yourself in Dept & Beds → ⚡ Quick Setup (see the note below). Either way the card returns to the selection UI showing the new current tier/seats.
Upgrades never remove existing capacity If a later request reduces the UG intake or PG seats compared to the current setup, approval will not delete existing beds or reduce sanctioned seats below what's already been approved historically. Downsizing capacity is not currently supported through this flow.
After an upgrade: set up the new beds in Quick Setup An intake or PG upgrade raises the bed requirement but does not create beds — the HMS can't know which physical ward or floor you'll put them in. Open Dept & Beds (bed-admin.html) → ⚡ Quick Setup: a banner shows how many beds each department is short, Table 1 is where you enter the new beds by type and building block, and Create All Beds places and numbers them. The NCISM compliance view will show the tenant below its required bed count until this is done.

↻ Renumber to Continuous Sequence — a button in Quick Setup (below the allocation table) that relabels every bed into one clean gap-free 1…N sequence, departments in fixed blocks (Panchakarma first, then Kayachikitsa, and so on). Only the bed number changes — wards, floors, bed types and current admissions are untouched. Optional: use it if you want a tidy audit roster where the highest number equals the bed count; skip it if your bed numbers are meant to reflect physical ward location. It is manual and one-click — run it after adding beds, before staff memorise the new numbers.

9. Feature Modules

Toggle which optional feature modules are visible/enabled for this tenant's staff (tied to the tenant's plan — some modules may be locked on lower plans; see the Plan Features checklist under Section 8.1).

🩺 OPD workflow (Session 347) — two switches, shown for every organisation. Screening OPD: ON = new patients and new complaints go to your Screening OPD, which sends them on to the specialty OPD (the NCISM teaching flow); OFF = reception chooses the OPD / department and the doctor directly, the OPD's fees load and the bill is made at the desk. Intern / PG verification: ON = interns and PG scholars record the case and press "Submit for Review"; a consultant verifies and completes it (the "Pending Review" tab); OFF = no "Submit for Review" — consultants complete every consultation. Not set = the default for your type of organisation: ON for a teaching hospital or college, OFF for a hospital, clinic or Panchakarma centre. Screening can be switched ON only when your organisation has an active Screening OPD (created by the NCISM setup in OPD Management; the Screening OPD is marked by the system, never chosen by name). Only your organisation's Super Admin can switch them (saved at once; every change is in the audit log); others see the current setting. Whatever the setting, reception never registers a visit without an OPD, and a visit registered with "No preference" for the doctor appears in the shared queue of every doctor on duty in that OPD today — the first doctor to open it takes it.

Counter sale (walk-in, no prescription) — shown above the module grid when your organisation has the pharmacy module. It is ON unless you switch it off. When ON, the pharmacist, cashier and admins can sell to a walk-in customer from Dispensary POS (NDPS, Schedule H1 and Schedule H medicines are always refused there). When OFF, only prescriptions can be dispensed. Only your organisation's Super Admin can switch it, using its own Switch ON / Switch OFF button (it is saved at once, not with "Save Changes"), and every change is recorded in the audit log.

🧾 Tax & Invoicing — also on this page (Super Admin, Dept Admin; Accountant and Finance Manager see it under Finance → 🧾 Tax settings): the IP-medicines choice, the default tax profiles and the room tax profiles (all three need a second person's approval), the document numbering (Super Admin), the Bill of Supply declaration (Super Admin; up to 300 characters, one line; empty = the standard Section 31(3)(c) / Rule 49 wording) and the pharmacy GST go-live date. The declaration is fixed on each Bill of Supply when it is issued and printed at its foot (it replaces the old "no GST is charged" note); later changes never alter issued bills.

GST readiness — the first part of the 🧾 Tax & Invoicing card. Pick a date and press Check: it lists what would stop GST billing going live on that date. Each line is marked ✖ Blocks, ⚠ Warning, ℹ Note or ✓ OK (the OK lines are folded under "N check(s) passed").

  • GST billing (decides go-live) — blocks when: the organisation is not marked GST-registered; the GSTIN, legal name, registered address or state code is missing; the state code is not the first two digits of the GSTIN; a demo organisation's legal name does not say DEMO; the default treatment-services profile is missing or not usable; or a tax profile your fee items use is not usable; or no IP-medicines choice (exempt or chargeable) is in force on the date, or the choice is EXEMPT but its profile is not exempt on that date (on a GST-live organisation either one stops IPD medicines being dispensed or billed on treatment admissions). "Usable" on the checked date means: your own, active, with a version approved and in force on that date, and not marked "Accounts review required". A fee item without its own tax profile is taxed through the default treatment-services profile, so if that default is not usable, one line under the default says how many fee items depend on it.
  • Also blocks: the room tax profiles (taxable rooms and exempt rooms) are not both set; a bed type in use has no room rate (or more than one active room rate) in fee-admin → IPD Room Tariff — the IPD bill is refused for such a bed; the document numbering has not been confirmed by the Super Admin.
  • Warnings (do not block): no default wellness-services profile.
  • Note: the room-rent rule itself (room rent above ₹5,000 per day per patient is taxable; ICU beds are always exempt).
  • Pharmacy — shown, never blocks GST go-live: stocked medicines without an HSN / tax record or an OP (sale) profile; under a CHARGEABLE choice with no fallback, stocked medicines without an IP profile (only their IPD bills are refused); and the pharmacy go-live date (which has its own check).

The same check runs on the server when the Super Admin sets or changes the GST go-live date: the date is refused while any GST line blocks, and the message names the blocking lines. An organisation that is already GST-live is not re-checked when anything else changes. The check is visible only to your own Super Admin, Dept Admin, Finance Manager and Accountant (active accounts).

Registration type (regular / composition) — part of the 🧾 Tax & Invoicing card, right under GST readiness. A GST-registered organisation is either regular or a composition taxable person; your CA confirms which, and whether your organisation is eligible. The card shows the type in force and its history. A change is sent for approval (a Super Admin or Finance Manager — never the person who asked, except an organisation's only Super Admin) and takes effect from the date you choose: composition only from 1 April (the intimation, CMP-02, is filed before the financial year) or, for a new registrant with no GST document yet, from its GST go-live date; back to regular from today or later (withdrawal, CMP-04 — crossing the turnover limit ends composition from that day). The CMP-02 / CMP-04 reference is required. Never back-dated, and a date on or before a GST document already issued is refused (every document keeps the type it was issued under). Until a GST-registered organisation has a registration type recorded, GST go-live is blocked. Under composition: every bill is a Bill of Supply with no GST on any line (each line keeps its category for the composition return), and the top of every Bill of Supply and credit note prints "Composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies"; receipts, refund vouchers, return slips and handover slips do not, and the exempt-supply declaration is not printed. Display the words "composition taxable person" on your signboards (Rule 5(1)(g)) — the card reminds you. The composition tax base (taxable supplies only, or taxable + exempt — your CA confirms it) is recorded the same way (a second person approves); it never changes a bill or a credit note and is used only by the GST reports, whose composition summary stays blocked until it is confirmed. A credit note dated under one type against a bill issued under the other is refused until your CA confirms how to treat it (a financial credit note remains possible). Composition turnover: the GST readiness check also shows a ⚠ Warning when this financial year's turnover (every bill incl. exempt, tax excluded, less credit notes and returns) reaches 80 % or 90 % of a composition limit, or crosses it (limits are platform values your CA confirms: ₹1.5 crore goods / ₹75 lakh in some states, ₹50 lakh services option) — crossing ends composition from that day; record the change to regular here. It never blocks. The figures are in Finance → 📑 GST returns.

Room rent tax (IPD) — part of the 🧾 Tax & Invoicing card. The rule is GST law and is the same for every organisation: room rent above ₹5,000 per day per patient is taxable; ICU beds are always exempt. It is set by the platform (your organisation cannot switch it off or change the amount) and should be confirmed by your CA before IPD GST goes live. Your organisation chooses only its room tax profiles: one for taxable rooms (a services profile, taxable), one for exempt rooms (a services profile, exempt or nil-rated) and, optionally, one for wellness / rejuvenation stays. A change is sent for approval (a Super Admin or Finance Manager — never the person who asked, except an organisation's only Super Admin) and takes effect from the date you choose, today or later — never back-dated. Each room-day of a stay is taxed by the profiles in force on that day, so a stay that crosses the change is split; bills already issued never change. The card also lists every bed type in use with its room rate and whether its rent is on the taxable or the exempt side.

Document numbering — Super Admin only. GST document numbers look like PREFIX/2627/000001 and must be at most 16 characters in all (GST Rule 46): prefix + "/" + 4-digit year + "/" + the number. So the prefix and the number of digits together can be at most 10 — prefixes are 1 to 5 characters (letters A–Z, digits 0–9 and hyphen; "/" is only the separator) and the number has 5 to 8 digits (6 by default; with 5, at most 99,999 documents of a type in a year). The six prefixes (Tax Invoice, Bill of Supply, Bill, Receipt, Credit note, Debit note) must all differ, and RTN, MC, IP and AYX are reserved for other series. The card shows each first number and its length as you type. A document type that has already issued numbers this financial year keeps its prefix and width until 31 March — the change applies to it from 1 April; numbers are never changed or reused, and a series that reaches its last number refuses clearly instead of wrapping round. The numbering must be confirmed once (press Confirm numbering, also to keep the defaults) — until then GST go-live is blocked.

🧮 Tax profiles — right under the 🧾 Tax & Invoicing card (Super Admin, Dept Admin; Accountant and Finance Manager under Finance → 🧾 Tax settings). A tax profile is the GST treatment of a group of items, e.g. “Medicines — Taxable 5%”: goods (HSN) or services (SAC); Taxable (with its rate), Exempt or Nil-rated. New profile / rate change: a Super Admin, Dept Admin, Finance Manager or Accountant makes it and sends it for approval; the rate is typed as a number and split equally (5% = CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%). A rate above 40% is refused; a rate that is not one of the usual GST rates (0.25, 3, 5, 18, 40 — a platform list, to be confirmed by your CA) must be ticked to confirm it. A rate change is a new version of the same profile that starts on a date you choose; the old version then ends the day before. Never back-dated: a version must start today or later — one that has already started can only be rejected. Approval: a Super Admin or Finance Manager approves or rejects it (with a reason) — never the person who made or sent it, except an organisation's only Super Admin (recorded as “self-approved (sole administrator)”). Only one version of a profile can wait for approval at a time. Profiles made by a migration (marked “Accounts review”) are confirmed by an approver with Confirm Accounts review. Retire (approver, with a reason) is offered only for a profile that is not in use; while a default, the room-tax rule, the IP-medicines choice, a medicine, a fee item or a package uses it, the screen lists those uses — move them to another profile first. The name can be changed until the first approval; the description at any time. Bills already issued keep their own tax and never change.

Credit notes (GST) — a finalised GST Tax Invoice or Bill of Supply is never edited, cancelled or deleted; a mistake is corrected by a credit note (and, if needed, a new bill). From this release a credit note can be issued against a fully paid, self-pay consultation, investigation or IPD bill (pharmacy bills come with the GST pharmacy returns; unpaid and insured bills later). An Accountant, Finance Manager or Super Admin asks for it — the whole bill or part of chosen lines, with the reason and how the money is refunded (cash, UPI, card, cheque or NEFT, with the reference) — and a second person (a Super Admin or Finance Manager, never the person who asked, except an organisation's only Super Admin) approves it in the "Tax settings waiting for approval" table. On approval the server re-checks everything, gives the note its number from the CN series (CN/2627/000001 …, in the year it is issued) and records the refund: a UPI / card / cheque / NEFT refund at once, as a new receipt-series entry with its reference; a cash refund becomes refund due — the person at the counter who hands over the money pays it out from their own shift (Finance → 🧾 Credit notes, or the IPD Account drawer) and a refund voucher prints; only that payout counts in a cash drawer or shift handover, and it can be paid out only once (for an IPD bill the refund goes through the admission's account, which shows the amount credited). The GST on each credited line is reversed at the original bill's own rates, never today's, and the last credit of a line takes exactly what is left, so a full credit reverses the tax to the paisa; nothing can be credited beyond what is left of a line or refunded beyond what was paid. A credit note against a Bill of Supply carries no GST; it is headed "CREDIT NOTE" and quotes the Bill of Supply's number and date. A GST credit note is allowed only until the earlier of 30 November after the end of the original bill's financial year and the date your organisation filed its GST annual return for that year (Section 34(2)). Your Super Admin records that filing date for each financial year (the screen comes with the credit-note screens; until it is recorded, 30 November applies). After the limit a GST credit note is refused. It counts in Finance as a return on the day it is issued; the original bill keeps its own day. A credit note itself can never be changed — a wrong one is corrected by another document. Where: an Accountant, Finance Manager or Super Admin presses ↶ Ask for a credit note on a paid GST bill in Visits & Bills (or on a paid IPD bill in the IPD Account drawer); the panel shows the time limit ("Credit allowed until …"), each line with what is already credited and what is left, and the server's own tax split, and refuses clearly after the limit. Every bill row shows the credit notes against it. Finance → 🧾 Credit notes lists every credit note, every request waiting for approval and every cash refund due (a number searches all dates). The credit note prints on A4, A5 or thermal 80 / 58 mm with the Rule 53 particulars, headed CREDIT NOTE, marked ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT (reprints: "REPRINT · ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT · No. N"), with an optional supplier copy ("DUPLICATE FOR SUPPLIER"). The original bill and its prints never change. The Super Admin records the GST annual return date per financial year in 🧾 Tax & Invoicing → GST annual return; a date earlier than a credit note already issued for that year is refused.

Printed copies of GST documents — a Tax Invoice prints ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT first and REPRINT · ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT · No. N after (A4 reprints also carry a "REPRINT" watermark). Its print page has an optional Print supplier copy button (finalised Tax Invoices only): the server labels it TRIPLICATE FOR SUPPLIER when the invoice has any medicine / goods line, otherwise DUPLICATE FOR SUPPLIER; the supplier copy has its own count (its first print is No. 1, later ones "REPRINT · … FOR SUPPLIER · No. N") and never changes the patient's copy number. There is no transporter copy. Every other document — Bills of Supply, plain bills, receipts, slips — keeps "Original" / "DUPLICATE COPY · No. N". GST documents show their document date, a recipient GSTIN when the bill has one, and the pharmacy bill is signed "Registered Pharmacist · Authorised Signatory". Every print is recorded by the server.

10. ABDM Bridge

Shows recent ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) callback activity — HIP linking, consent requests, and health data exchange events routed through the tenant's ABDM integration. AyurXpert's own ABDM Bridge certification (Sandbox ID SBXID_033899) already covers every tenant on the platform — you do not need any certification of your own. What your organisation does still need, one time, is its own Health Facility Registry (HFR) Facility ID, covered in 10.1 below.

10.1 Activating ABDM Services (HFR Facility Registration)

Until this is done, every ABDM function is blocked for your organisation — this includes basic ABHA verification/enrollment, not just Scan & Share or consent-based data exchange. This is a deliberate, complete block (8 Aug 2026) rather than a partial one: nothing about ABDM works until your Facility ID is registered, full stop. Your organisation's normal HMS operation (patients, visits, prescriptions, billing) is entirely unaffected — it never depended on ABDM. This is entirely self-service — super_admin only, no platform admin involvement needed.

  1. 1
    Register with India's Health Facility Registry (HFR) — go to facility.abdm.gov.in and register your hospital using its own PAN/license details. You'll receive a Facility ID (e.g. IN2910XXXXXX). You don't need to wait for full "Validated" status — "Submitted" is enough for ABDM services to work.
  2. 2
    Open ABDM Bridge Configuration — in admin.html's sidebar, click 🏛️ ABDM Bridge (Section 10 above).
  3. 3
    Enter your Facility ID — in the "This Organisation's HFR Facility Registration" panel, paste the Facility ID from Step 1. Optionally set a Display Name (max 15 characters) — this is what patients see when searching for your facility on the ABHA app.
  4. 4
    Click "Save & Register with ABDM" — this does two things in one click: saves the Facility ID to your organisation's record, and registers it live with ABDM's own systems as a service under AyurXpert's Bridge. Both are required — the first alone is not enough for ABDM to recognize your facility.
  5. 5
    Confirm — the status message should read "✅ Registered — Facility ID … is now live on AyurXpert's ABDM Bridge." ABDM services are active for your organisation from this point, no further setup or waiting required.
A Facility ID can only belong to one organisation on the platform If you enter a Facility ID already registered to another tenant, Save will be rejected with a clear message naming the conflicting organisation — double-check the ID from your HFR registration email/portal before entering it here.
Correcting a wrong Facility ID, or deactivating entirely If you save a different Facility ID than the one currently registered, the previous one is automatically withdrawn from AyurXpert's ABDM Bridge — no orphaned registration is left behind. To stop ABDM services for your organisation entirely (without switching to a different Facility ID), use the 🚫 Deactivate button next to Save — this withdraws the current Facility ID from the Bridge and clears the local record. Re-enter a Facility ID and save again any time to reactivate.

Check Section 8.1 for the exact expiry date and current plan/billing cycle, then contact support@ayurxpert.com — renewal itself is applied by the platform admin, not self-service.

Go to Subscription → NCISM Capacity Plan, select the 100 tier card, adjust any PG seats if needed, review the total fee, and Submit Request. After approval, open Dept & Beds → ⚡ Quick Setup to add the extra IPD beds the higher intake requires — the approval raises the requirement but does not create beds. Go to Human Resources → find their profile → use the "Reset MFA" action (super_admin only). This clears their enrolled factor so they can re-enroll on next login.
