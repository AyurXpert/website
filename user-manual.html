System Overview

Your Day, Role by Role

The OPD day in the order you work it. Each step says what to click and what you should see; the fuller detail is in the section linked from each guide.

Receptionist
Find the patient
Type a mobile number, name or UHID in ① Find patient. You see: the patient card with the ABHA number · address, or nothing for a new patient. Details: Reception.
Fill the visit
A new patient or a new complaint goes to the Screening OPD where the hospital has one: the Doctor box says — assigned at Screening —. A follow-up goes to the previous OPD.
Collect and register
Check the fee the server chose and its reason in ④ Payment, take the payment, click Register & Create Visit. You see: the token, and the bill or receipt to print. Send the patient to the Screening desk (or the OPD).
Watch the warning
A red line under the tabs of Today's work, "⚠ N patients waiting at Screening — no counter active. Tell the Medical Superintendent.", means no screener is working. Tell the MS.
Wrong doctor or OPD
Click ✎ on the queue card. On a visit routed by Screening the Doctor and Visit Type boxes are greyed and only the complaint can change: ask the Screening counter to Re-route. On any other visit, changing the doctor asks for a reason.
Wrong name, mobile or age
Click ✎ Correct patient details and give a reason. Reception may correct while the patient has a visit today not yet consulted; after that it is Medical Records or the Super Admin.
End of shift
Click 🤝 Close my shift, then Logout.
Screener — nurse, doctor or intern on Screening duty

This guide covers Screening only. A nurse's ward work is on nursing.html.

Open Screening OPD
Click Screening OPD in the menu. You see: the counter box. If you see "You are not on Screening duty today. Ask the Medical Superintendent.", you are not on today's list: ask the MS, Deputy MS or RMO to add you.
Start your counter
Check the counter name and click 🟢 Start Triaging. You see: "On duty at Counter 1" and your share of the waiting patients.
Choose from the queue
At the top: ↩ Returned by … (a doctor sent the patient back; every counter sees it). Below: your own patients, and ⏱ Waiting N min (waited too long; every counter sees it). The list refreshes every 20 seconds.
Screen and route
Click the patient → choose the triage level → enter complaint and vitals → choose the OPD (and doctor, or leave it for the OPD's shared queue) → ↗ Route to …. You see: "… routed to Kayachikitsa OPD — token #4".
Routed to the wrong OPD?
Click 🔁 Routed today, not yet seen above the queue → Re-route… beside the patient → choose the OPD and doctor → give the reason (5 to 300 characters) → Re-route. You see: "Re-routed to … — token #N". This works only before the doctor opens the visit; after that the doctor presses ↩ Return to Screening.
A returned patient
Open the ↩ Returned patient, read the doctor's reason on the badge, and route again as above.
Leaving the counter
Click 🔴 Stop Triaging. You see: your waiting patients move to the counters still active. Details: Screening OPD.

On the doctor page
You land on doctor.html with your posting department's queue. Open a patient and record the case; you can order investigations.

Screening — interns only
An intern on today's duty list screens like any screener, but must choose the supervising doctor before routing; the name is recorded. PG scholars do not screen.
Patient in the wrong OPD
You cannot press ↩ Return to Screening yourself: the server allows only a consultant. Ask the visit's doctor, a doctor on duty in that OPD, or the Medical Superintendent.

4a. Tax & Invoicing

Where: Admin → Settings → 🧾 Tax & Invoicing (Super Admin, Dept Admin)  |  Finance → 🧾 Tax settings (Accountant, Finance Manager). Cashiers, pharmacists and other staff do not see it.

Changes to the IP-medicines choice and the default tax profiles need a second person's approval (a Super Admin or Finance Manager — never the person who asked, except an organisation's only Super Admin). Bills already issued never change.

IP medicines (treatment admissions)
Shows the choice in force and its history. To change it: choose EXEMPT (pick the exempt goods profile) or CHARGEABLE (each medicine's own IP profile; optionally a fallback profile for medicines without one), the date it starts (today or later — never back-dated) and a reason, then "Send for approval". While it waits, the card shows "⏳ Pending approval". With no choice for a date, an IPD bill or an IPD dispense with medicines is refused on a GST-live organisation. Under CHARGEABLE, a medicine with no IP profile (and no fallback) is refused at the bill, never at the dispense.
Default tax profiles
The default profile for OP medicines (goods, HSN), treatment services and wellness services (SAC). Change one or more; the preview shows old → new; "Send for approval" sends one request.
Registration type — regular or composition (Session 345f)
Your CA confirms whether the organisation is a regular GST registration or a composition taxable person. Record it in Registration type (a second person approves; the CMP-02 / CMP-04 reference is required). Composition starts only on 1 April, or on a new registrant's GST go-live date; going back to regular is from today or later. Never back-dated; a date on or before an issued GST document is refused. Under composition every bill is a Bill of Supply with no GST and carries "Composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies" at the top (credit notes too; not receipts or slips). The card reminds you to display "composition taxable person" on your signboards. The composition tax base (taxable only / taxable + exempt) is recorded the same way and is used only by the GST reports.
Bill of Supply declaration (Super Admin)
The line printed at the foot of every Bill of Supply. Up to 300 characters, one line, no < or >; a counter and a live preview show what will print. "Use default" returns to the standard wording ("Bill of Supply under Section 31(3)(c) of the CGST Act, 2017 read with Rule 49 — exempt supply; no GST is charged on this bill."). The text is fixed on each bill when it is issued — later changes never alter issued bills. Other roles see the text read-only.
Printed copies of GST documents (Session 345c2b)
A Tax Invoice prints ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT the first time and REPRINT · ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT · No. N afterwards (header and footer; an A4 reprint also carries a "REPRINT" watermark). A finalised Tax Invoice also has an optional Print supplier copy button on the print page: the server labels it TRIPLICATE FOR SUPPLIER when the invoice has any medicine / goods line, otherwise DUPLICATE FOR SUPPLIER; it has its own count (the first supplier copy is print 1; later ones say "REPRINT · … FOR SUPPLIER · No. N") and never changes the patient's copy number. There is no transporter copy. A Bill of Supply prints its declaration (the text fixed on it when it was issued) at the foot, in place of the old "no GST is charged" note — bills issued before this have none and keep the note. Bills of Supply, plain bills, receipts and slips keep "Original" / "DUPLICATE COPY · No. N". Every GST document shows its document date; a recipient GSTIN prints when the bill has one; the pharmacy bill is signed "Registered Pharmacist · Authorised Signatory".
Finding a bill by its number (Session 345c2c)
🔎 Visits & Bills (Reception, Finance, Dispensary) finds any bill or invoice of your organisation by its number — B/…, a Tax Invoice TI/…, a Bill of Supply BS/… (and any prefix your organisation's documents use), a receipt RCPT/… or a return slip RTN/… — across ALL dates. A UHID still finds the patient. Only your own organisation's documents are ever found.
IPD medicines when a tax setting needs fixing (Session 345c2c)
A tax setting never stops treatment. If the IP-medicines choice is EXEMPT but its tax profile is not exempt on the day, the pharmacist still issues the medicines: the Review issue to the IPD stay panel shows the warning under "Before you hand it over", and the Issued panel repeats it. The IPD bill is refused until an administrator corrects the tax profile or the IP-medicines choice here. Only when there is no IP-medicines choice at all is the dispense itself refused.
Pharmacy GST go-live (Super Admin)
The date your pharmacy starts GST billing. The server refuses it, with the reason shown on the card, until the pharmacy is ready (every stocked medicine has an HSN record and an approved sale profile, the IP-medicines choice is recorded, the date is not before your GST go-live or in the past). Pharmacy GST stays off platform-wide until credit notes (Stage 6) are ready.
Deciding a request
Finance → 🧾 Tax settings (top table) or HR → ⚖️ Approvals (the "Tax settings waiting for approval" table): each request shows who asked, why, and old → new. Approve, or Reject with a reason (at least 5 characters). The badge on the Finance tab counts every tax decision waiting for you (these two kinds and medicine tax changes).

This is where every patient's day begins. You find or register the patient, the server works out the fee, you collect the payment, and the patient joins the doctor's queue. The steps below follow the order of a working day: what to click, then what you should see.

1. Open the page — what is where
The two panes
Click: nothing yet. You see: Registration (teal) on the left and Today's work (blue) on the right. In NCISM teaching hospitals and colleges the purple NCISM OPD strip sits above them. The slim line under the strip shows your registration duty, 🤝 Close my shift and the Layout buttons.
Pull out / Push in
Click: Pull out ⤢ on the Registration pane, then ⇤ Push in; the same on Today's work (⤢ Pull out / Push in ⇥). You see: that pane fills the screen and the other becomes a thin coloured strip with its counts — click the strip to bring it back. The pane header with these buttons stays just below the top bar while you scroll. Side by side shows the two panes at equal width. Your layout is remembered for you on any computer.
2. The NCISM OPD strip (teaching hospitals / colleges)

Counted by the server, the same way everywhere (doctor page, NCISM Compliance, SDF): each patient once per day, new and follow-up; Screening + the specialty visit = one patient; lab-only walk-ins, tele-consultations, cancelled / deleted visits and no-shows are not counted; an emergency counts once a doctor has seen it.

Look at the strip
You see: Today — the target (UG intake × 2), Seen today (NCISM) (new · follow-up — a patient counts once they have reached the Screening desk or a doctor), Still needed today, Registered today, still waiting (registered but not yet seen — not part of the NCISM figure), how many more to reach the line, the % bar and the 75 % line (the hospital's own alert line, not an NCISM rule); and the 12-month average (patients of the last 12 months ÷ 300 days, NCISM Reg. 42 Table-7; a hospital that started less than a year ago sees the average per OPD day since then; averages show one decimal, e.g. 0.3 / day).
📅 Date range
Click: 📅 Date range, then a Quick range (Today, This week, This month, Last month, Rolling 12 months — and the academic year once the Super Admin has set its start month) or type From / To and press Show. If you change a date by hand the Quick range box shows — custom —. You see: five cards — OPD days (weekly off and holidays left out), Target for period, Seen (NCISM) (new · follow-up; patients seen on a Sunday or holiday still count), the Average per OPD day (the NCISM figure) and days at or above 75 % with the shortfall — and a bar for every day with its number on top: green at or above 75 %, orange striped below, grey = not an OPD day.
A day's patients

Who Can Publish Changes — the Nursing Head

By default, whoever holds the Nursing Superintendent designation is the "Nursing Head" and the only one (besides super_admin/dept_admin) who can generate, publish or edit the roster. Generating a Week's Roster (recommended path)

Use Generate Roster (nursing-roster-generate.html) rather than assigning shifts one at a time — it solves an entire period at once: rotating weekly-off, covering gaps from within the same department first, then a small set of clinically-sensible cross-department pairings (e.g. Screening OPD ↔ Panchakarma), and only reports an honest, visible gap if truly nobody is free.

Pick the period

This is the actual finalise step — there's no further "approve" or "confirm" stage after this.
