Instructions for the Patient
  1. You have been given a Virechana (purgation) medicine. You will pass motion several times through the day — this is expected and part of the treatment.
  2. Each time you pass motion, please fill one row in the table on the next page, before flushing:
  3. Note the time you passed motion.
  4. Circle how much — Small, Medium or Large (a small cup, a full cup, or more than a cup).
  5. Look at the Consistency chart below and circle the number that looks closest.
  6. Look at the Type chart below and circle what it looked like.
  7. When you are done for the day, or if you need any help filling this, hand this sheet to the nurse, PG doctor, or doctor on duty.
⚠ If you feel dizzy, faint, have severe stomach pain, vomiting, or see blood — STOP and call the nurse IMMEDIATELY. Do not wait to finish filling this form.
